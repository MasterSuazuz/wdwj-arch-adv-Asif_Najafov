How Dasturxon works

Dasturxon is organized around a single office that coordinates managers, kitchens, and couriers, so every order gets the same attention from request to doorstep — no matter which neighborhood you're in.

When you reach out, a manager sets up your subscription and creates your order. The kitchen prepares each dish around your allergy notes, and a courier from your office handles the delivery and keeps the status up to date.
