The application is a food delivery subscription platform that allows clients to purchase and manage meal subscriptions with regular courier deliveries. Clients can submit requests, choose a subscription, make payments, and manage their orders. Managers process client requests and payments and organize orders, while kitchens and couriers are responsible for preparing and delivering meals.

Users: Client — chooses a subscription, submits requests, makes payments, views their orders, tracks deliveries. Manager — receives client requests, processes payments, creates and manages orders, coordinates dishes and deliveries. Courier — receives delivery assignments, performs deliveries, updates delivery status. Kitchen — supplies/prepares dishes required for orders. Office — coordinates managers, kitchens and courier

Basic scenario:
1. Client opens the application and chat with manager.
2. Manager shows available meal subscriptions.
3. Client selects a subscription.
4. Client submits a request.
5. Client makes the payment.
6. Manager processes the request and creates the order.
7. The order receives dishes and a delivery.
8. Courier delivers the order.
9. Client can view the delivery status.

Advanced scenario:
1. Client opens the application and views their active subscription.
2. Client opens an upcoming order.
3. Client views the dishes included in the order and the planned delivery.
4. Client submits a request to change the order, such as changing delivery details or specifying food allergies..
5. Manager receives and reviews the client's request.
6. Manager updates the order according to the request.
7. Kitchen receives the updated dish information.
8. Courier receives the updated delivery information.
9. Client checks the updated order and delivery status.
